# Product Brief: VERBAMIND

**Project Title**: VERBAMIND — AI-Powered English Speaking & Placement Communication Coaching  
**Category**: Final Year B.Tech (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning) Project  
**Author / Developer**: Yasir  
**Target Duration**: 6 Months (~5 hours daily)  
**Status**: Active — Phase 1 (Foundation & Core Recording Loop)  

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Engineering graduates and job applicants in non-native English regions often face barriers during placement interviews, not due to lack of technical competence, but due to hesitation, uneven speaking pace, excessive filler words, articulation breakdowns, and lack of objective, non-judgmental feedback.

Existing commercial solutions either:
1. Offer passive grammar quizzes without speaking practice.
2. Rely entirely on opaque LLM text generation that guesses speaking quality from transcripts alone without analyzing raw acoustic speech signals.
3. Conflate grammatical syntax with acoustic fluency, or pronunciation with accent.
4. Fail to offer side-by-side retry comparisons showing demonstrable acoustic improvement.

---

## 2. Solution: VERBAMIND
VERBAMIND is a web-based, AI/ML-assisted communication coaching platform designed specifically for placement readiness. It provides a structured training loop:
```
Choose Exercise ──> Record Speech ──> Process Acoustics & Transcript ──> Receive Evidence-Linked Feedback ──> Targeted Practice ──> Retry & Compare Improvement
```

### Key Pillars
- **Acoustic & Linguistic Grounding**: Measures true vocal delivery (words per minute, pause distribution, filler sound frequency, acoustic energy) alongside linguistic reading accuracy and pronunciation confidence.
- **Evidence-Linked Feedback**: Users don't just see a score; they see the exact timestamps and transcript tokens where pauses lingered, fillers occurred, or mispronunciations were detected.
- **Iterative Comparison**: Allows candidates to record take-two and visually inspect their delta (e.g. "Filler count reduced from 7 to 2; speaking pace stabilized from 185 WPM to 142 WPM").
- **Research-Grade Custom Fluency Model**: A dedicated ML model trained and evaluated specifically on unseen speakers to guarantee objective acoustic scoring without data leakage.
